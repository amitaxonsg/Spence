## Mission and differentiation
We are not a conventional break-fix IT support shop. SpenceCom is a technology modernisation and systems integration consultancy, led by more than two decades of IT consulting experience. We connect the foundations people already depend upon—LAN, structured cabling, Wi-Fi, NAS, servers, AV and security—to cloud services, intelligent controls and practical AI.

**Do not replace what works merely to sell hardware.** Audit and preserve suitable existing infrastructure, modernise in stages, and build new systems from scratch where necessary. Collaborate with the client's current IT, facilities, electrical and maintenance providers: SpenceCom can act as design and integration specialist without taking over routine support.

Positioning: **Your existing technology has more potential. We help unlock it.**
Tagline: **Smart Homes. Connected Workplaces. AI-Ready Futures.**
Invitation: **Talk to us for a free introductory consultation.**
Credibility: **International experience. Local delivery. Documented results.**
Do not promise guaranteed ROI, dramatic savings, universal device compatibility or real-world intelligence without testing.

## AI readiness is not a device label
Begin with stable connectivity, segmented IoT networks, identity, access policy, backups, predictable management and documented integration interfaces. AI features are optional and must solve an identified workflow. Video analytics, automated decisions and assistants require explicit review of accuracy, security, privacy, processing location, licences and ongoing cost. Never connect private company documents to a public demonstration chatbot.

## Proposals and packages
- **Smart Home Foundations**: Wi-Fi/network audit plus selected lighting, security or climate-control integrations.
- **Smart Living Complete**: scoped design for whole-property scenes, media, cameras and convenience controls, with manual fallback.
- **Smart Office Upgrade**: preserve working LAN/NAS/AV, introduce reliable connectivity, meeting tools and approved automations.
- **Hospitality Connected Guest**: coverage design, segmented staff/guest Wi-Fi and optional branded guest portal.
- **AI-Ready Infrastructure Assessment**: evaluate existing LAN/Wi-Fi/NAS/identity/cloud/data access and provide phased roadmap.
- **Private Knowledge Assistant Pilot**: one permission-aware knowledge use case with measured acceptance criteria.
- **New Site Ready from Day One**: integrated design for cabling, Wi-Fi, storage, AV, access and future automation.
- **Ongoing Care**: scoped management, security and lifecycle checks—not unlimited general-purpose IT support.

Pricing should separate discovery, design, hardware, specialist labour, implementation, subscriptions and optional care. Free consultation means introductory conversation; detailed audits or site visits may be chargeable.

## Existing-provider collaboration
We welcome work alongside the client's incumbent IT administrator, electricians, building manager, maintenance contractor, AV vendor or security provider. Define change ownership, escalation, credentials, network boundaries, outage windows, warranties and written handover. SpenceCom supplies system architecture and integration expertise; routine IT support need not be displaced.

## Guardrails
- Use qualified trades for line-voltage electrical works and regulated fire/life-safety systems; do not automate safety-critical functions without appropriate specialist validation.
- Keep manual override and graceful operation when Wi-Fi, controller or cloud fails.
- Avoid exposing IoT control systems to the public internet; use secure access, segmentation and MFA where supported.
- Vet Matter, Thread, Zigbee, Wi-Fi and vendor cloud interoperability device-by-device; never suggest all ecosystems always work together.
- Do not promise AI-native Wi-Fi: market Wi-Fi optimisation/analytics features by their actual tested functionality, not generic AI branding.
- Captive portals must protect guest information and offer appropriate notices; don't claim unrestricted tracking.
- Obtain informed consent for recording/transcription and respect camera/data privacy.
- Distinguish consultancy leadership's 20+ years from the age of the new SpenceCom business.
