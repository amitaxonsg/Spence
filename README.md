## 2. Customers and outcomes

Initial focus: business owners, office managers and operations teams opening an office, moving premises, expanding, suffering unreliable Wi-Fi or struggling with fragmented files and manual work.

Potential segments: professional offices, manufacturers, multi-department organisations, growing SMEs and businesses with remote or overseas management. Confirm the initial service geography before advertising nationwide onsite coverage.

Sell outcomes:
- Reliable connectivity and usable Wi-Fi coverage.
- Organised company files with appropriate employee access.
- Meetings where people can join, hear, present and collaborate easily.
- Secure access between office systems and cloud services.
- Less repetitive administration and faster access to company knowledge.
- Clear ownership, documentation and ongoing maintenance.

Avoid disparaging local technicians or claiming the whole market is outdated. Demonstrate the difference through delivery quality.

## 4. Scope boundaries

Core: assessment, solution design, installation, integration, commissioning, employee enablement and agreed ongoing management.

Equipment supply and configuration are included when required for the integrated solution. General PC repair, motherboard repair, printer repair and walk-in troubleshooting are outside the core offer.

Electrical installation, specialist building work and other regulated tasks use suitably qualified contractors. AV specialists can assist with complex rooms. Define partner responsibilities in each proposal.

Do not sell unbounded “all IT support.” Contracts define systems covered, hours, response targets, remote versus onsite service, incident limits, exclusions and third-party dependencies.

## 5. Commercial offers

Assess existing infrastructure; identify faults; upgrade cabling and equipment where justified; implement Wi-Fi and network controls; test and document.

### B. Connected Workplace
Network foundation plus NAS, departmental access, cloud integration, remote access and backup/recovery.

### C. Smart Meeting Room
Three levels: Connected (clear meetings and presentation), Collaborative (interactive whiteboard and shared work), Intelligent (supported AI records and approved workflow integration).

### D. Intelligent Workplace
A focused pilot for a company document assistant or workflow automation, followed by measured expansion.

### E. New Office, Ready to Work
Coordinate network layout, cable routes, racks, Wi-Fi, meeting spaces, storage and cloud access with the office fit-out programme.

### F. Managed Workplace Care
Recurring maintenance and monitoring for the deployed systems, with a defined service schedule.

Pricing structure: free introductory consultation; separately scoped paid assessment where needed; fixed installation/integration scope; transparent equipment and recurring licences; optional monthly care. Deduct an assessment fee from a larger project only where commercially agreed.

Do not publish invented savings, fixed prices or “unlimited AI.” Measure baseline task time, recurring costs and usage before estimating benefits.

## 6. Delivery method and quality promise

1. Discover: business goals, employee count, premises, problems, current systems and constraints.
2. Assess: inspect connectivity, cabling, Wi-Fi, storage, rooms, permissions and dependencies.
3. Design: proposed architecture, retained equipment, phases, risks, responsibilities and acceptance criteria.
4. Quote: separate equipment, labour, software, recurring fees, exclusions and optional work.
5. Implement: backups, agreed downtime, controlled changes and rollback planning.
6. Verify: test the agreed employee workflows and technical requirements.
7. Enable: train staff and nominate client administrators.
8. Handover: diagrams, labels, inventory, access ownership, configurations and operating guides.
9. Maintain: optional monitoring, updates, recovery checks and improvement reviews.

Proof must match the service: measured network results, authorised access checks, an actual restore, a remote participant meeting and a chatbot permission test. A speed-test screenshot alone is not acceptance for the entire project.

## 7. Private company assistant architecture

Approved documents in NAS/cloud repositories -> permission-aware ingestion -> protected search index -> authenticated employee assistant -> source-linked answers.

Requirements:
- Confirm document owners, approved sources and permissions before ingestion.
- Enforce access at retrieval time; avoid exposing restricted content through search, summaries or caches.
- Update and remove indexed content when source permissions or files change.
- Show source references and acknowledge insufficient evidence.
- Separate read-only document questions from actions such as emailing or changing records.
- Require explicit human approval for consequential actions.
- Document hosting, external processors, retention, audit logging and deletion.
- Test cross-department access, stale documents and malicious instructions inside documents.
- Set usage budgets and measure cost per useful task.

Launch the website with a demonstration using synthetic or approved public sample documents. A public website chatbot must not access internal client documents.
